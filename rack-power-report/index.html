A meter name can end in * to cover many meters at once, e.g. PDU-S1-FN2-R27*. This list is only used when the data file has no rack column of its own. Meters that are not listed appear under “Unassigned”. A third column (company) is optional: it gives one meter a company directly. The sample data comes with example lists, which are cleared when you load your own file.
Each company here becomes a button. A company's report uses the meters of its racks: meter → rack (list 1, or the rack column of the file) → company (list 2). A rack name can use *, e.g. R2*.
